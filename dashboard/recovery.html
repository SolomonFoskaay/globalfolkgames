Points Recovery

Diagnose and recover orphaned M3 local points that never credited to M4 global ledgers.

How it works: Search a player by email or wallet address. The tool reads their on-chain M3 local points (per game) and M4 global points, compares totals, and shows any orphaned points that never reached the global ledger. One click to fix (all gasless, no manual point entry).
Enter a player email or wallet address above to begin.
When a player contacts support about missing points, ask for:
  • Their email address (the one they sign in with)
  • Or their wallet address (shown on their profile page)
  • Which game they played (Ludo, Ayo Olopon, etc.)
  • When they played (approximate date/time)
  • What position they finished (1st, 2nd, etc.)
The tool will auto-detect orphaned points and offer a one-click fix.
