Earn Competition (AGM)

AGM means Automated Game Matchmaking: a first-of-its-kind idea for games, invented by the founder of GlobalFolkGames, Solomon Foskaay. It borrows the best of the trading world (CEX and DEX) and turns a gameplay match into something the players can open, take, and settle, just like a token or an NFT. The point is simple: native gamers show their prowess against players worldwide, and the winner of each match earns a share of the competition pot. Every step is recorded on a chain you can check.

How it works (the plain version)

  1. Post a match: pick your game and the prize level. This writes your open match on-chain, so it is public and provable.
  2. Take a match: another player (or the computer backer) accepts your open match. You can never accept your own match.
  3. Play: two players, one winner. The prize pool is locked the moment the match is taken.
  4. Settle: the winner's share is finalized on-chain and ready to be paid out.

The share split is shown clearly on each match before you take it. There is no hidden maths, and the platform share is printed up front so anyone can see it.

Post a match

This is you opening a match for other players to take. You are the maker.

When someone takes your match, the winner's share is locked and settled on-chain. The platform share is shown on each open match.

Take a match (the other side)

An open match on the Open matches tab is waiting for someone to take it. Taking it commits you as the opponent, and the winner's share is locked for you both. You can never take your own match, and you only ever pay with the same coin the maker used, so the deal is always fair and single-currency.

Matches with a (Computer backer) tag are filled by the platform's own pool when no human is ready, so a match is never left waiting. That pool is managed by the team and players never fund it.

Staff manage the backer pool from the dashboard; it is never shown to players.

Words you will see

AGM = Automated Game Matchmaking, the system that opens, takes and settles matches.
P2P = Player vs Player, both seats are real humans.
P2C = Player vs Computer, a computer seat fills the match so no one waits.
Pot = the prize pool: every player's prize level added together.
Open / Locked / Settled = an open match is waiting for a taker; locked means two players are committed; settled means the winner is finalized.
