AGM Arena (Arc 2.0 earn lobby)

Post an order, match it, lock the pot, settle at the 10% flat fee. Every write is relay/sponsor signed on your behalf. Orders come from a live on-chain registry; statuses are read from Solana, never from a database.

How it works (with the numbers from our devnet tests)

  1. Post: pick a game (Ludo = 1), a stake ($5), and seats (2). This writes your ticket on-chain.
  2. Match: another wallet (or the test taker) accepts. Your wallet can never accept its own ticket.
  3. Lock: the pot is fixed. 2 seats x $5 = a $10 pot, $1 fee (10%), winner gets $9.
  4. Settle: finalizes the order so the wallet rail can pay the winner.

The P2C panel below handles the computer bank: fund it, then apply a computer-seat result to a locked settlement (computer win +payout-stake, loss -stake). The bank pauses for the day if it loses $20+.
