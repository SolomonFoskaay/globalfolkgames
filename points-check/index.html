On-chain points viewer

Reads the M3 local points ledger and M4 global ledgers straight off the blockchain, exactly like the recovery tool does.

What you'll see: this page does no caching and no sign-in-based math. It asks the blockchain directly: "what does this wallet's M3 and M4 record say?" A wallet that has never banked on-chain will show a clearly marked "no account yet" state (empty record, not a bug). A wallet that has banked shows the exact pure / lifetime / spendable numbers recorded on-chain.
Enter a wallet address (or click "Use my wallet") to read its on-chain points.
