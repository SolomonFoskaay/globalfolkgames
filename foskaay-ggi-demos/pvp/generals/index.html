FOSKAAY GGI DEMO

Generals, fully on-chain on Arc

A real strategy game ported to Arc with its own contract. The board lives on-chain, every move is checked by the chain, and your wallet pays nothing. The sponsor covers the tiny USDC gas.

Press New match to open a session and start.

Tap one of your blue cells, then tap an adjacent cell to move strength into it. Attacking a red or neutral cell takes it over when your damage beats its strength. Reinforcing your own cell adds the strength.

On-chain activity

What this proves. The game's board is its OWN contract on Arc, not browser state. Every move calls the contract, and the contract checks the Foskaay GGI session before it accepts a move. The rail underneath is Foskaay Gasless Games Infrastructure, and you can see every session in the Foskaay GGI Explorer. The game is a port of MagicBlock's open-source Generals sample, with their graphics.

How the cost works, plainly. Arc has no free execution layer, so every move in this demo is a real on-chain transaction. The sponsor (the game operator) pays it, never you. Batching (the optional Foskaay GGI pattern) folds the final settlement of many matches into one on-chain stamp; it does not remove the per-move writes. A game that keeps its board off-chain during play and settles once pays far less.
