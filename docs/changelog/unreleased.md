- Value model (mainnet phase, agreed shape): "free today, honest about limits." Free tier = a daily renewing sponsor-cost allowance per player, metered by real sponsor balance-delta from the spend ledger (not arbitrary roll counts) and shown to the player as "free plays left today".
- Top-up: players can fund their embedded Solana wallet (Dynamic on-ramp) and buy spendable points; points unlock extra allowance and specials (lives, tournament re-entries). Earned and purchased points share the existing point_transactions ledger (different reason tags).
- Early refill: during the onboarding phase players can request a free allowance refill from their profile (owner grants). The limit is taught from day one so later removal of free refills is never a surprise.
- Transparency: per-game sponsor cost, allowance used, wins and turns are tracked and visible on both the user profile and the admin dashboard, so the owner can see how far the free gas budget stretches (games/turns per SOL).
- Competition fairness: competitions are never pay-to-enter; each event bundles an allowance sized to finish it. Paid pressure applies to extra/grinding play, never to locking a player out of an event they already entered.

- Scope A (all dice on-chain): EVERY dice roll resolves on the MagicBlock ER VRF (fast gasless queue) - not just the first user proof roll. Human seat rolls via the player's delegated dice PDA on every turn; computer seats roll via a new server-side house-key route (POST /api/roll on the local relay + api/roll.mjs on Vercel) - the house dice account is sponsored+delegated once (~0.0013 SOL, idempotent), key never leaves the server, rolls serialized in-process, delegation cached ~4min (warm ~1.3s, cold ~3.1s; under the 2.5s p95 revert rule). Any seat falls back to local randomness only if VRF/relay is unreachable so play never hard-stalls.
