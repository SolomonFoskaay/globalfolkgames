How the monthly Member Cup works

  1. Play free whenever you want. Wins still write to your ledger. Free play never shares membership fees.
  2. Want this month’s share? Be on L1, L2, or L3 Early Backer, tick that you are 18+, and press Join on this month’s cup. You are not auto-enrolled. Miss the button, miss the month.
  3. Play completed games in the window. First place is 100 pure game-win points. In a 4-player game, 2nd is 25, 3rd is 10, 4th is 0. Abandoned and reset games pay nothing. Computer, a friend on the same phone, or a stranger all count in this version - we care that you showed up and finished.
  4. Hit 10,000 raw points before any boost. That is about 100 first-place finishes in the 30-day window. Below 10,000 you stay on the public board. You do not enter the share.
  5. Final Points = raw points × your plan. L1 × 1.00. L2 × 1.25. L3 Early Backer × 1.50. Qualifiers split 50% of membership fees collected that same month, in proportion to Final Points.
  6. Stay subscribed through the last day. Let the plan lapse and you drop off the paid board until you renew inside the window. When the window closes, it closes. Payouts land in the embedded wallet 1 to 7 days later. Under $5 waits for the next month.

No second entry fee. Your plan is the ticket.

What 50% actually means

The share is not a prize we top up from a private pocket. If 40 people pay L1 this month, 50% of those fees is the cup. If 400 people pay, the cup is ten times that. You see the live pool number on the cup card when a window is open. After close, the same page shows who qualified, their Final Points, and the paid transaction.

Ads, lives packs, and cosmetic boards stay with the studio. Brand-sponsored side cups, when they exist, are separate and labelled.

Questions

Is this gambling?

No stake against another player. The month’s membership fees fund a published ranking. If your country cannot take cash, you still play and rank.

Can I join late?

Yes, until the window ends. Only wins inside the window count. 10,000 is 10,000 whether you start on day 1 or day 10.

What if I only play vs computer?

It counts for this monthly cup. Finish the game.

What if I forget to tap Join?

You play for the ledger and for fun. You do not share this month.

Why 10,000?

So the 50% goes to people who lived on the board, not to a hundred accounts with one lucky game.

When do I get paid?

1-7 days after the month ends, to the same embedded wallet you use on the site.
