How it works

  1. Enter a window - be on one of the plans the competition allows, and pay the small entry once.
  2. Play inside the window - only verified wins made while it is live count toward the board. The window stops accepting results the moment it ends.
  3. Watch Final Points - your board score is Total Points, boosted live by your plan (Level 3 = 1.5x, Level 2 = 1x). Downgrade mid-window and the boost recalculates instantly; drop to the free plan and you go hidden until you resubscribe.
  4. Top finishers share the pool - the winners are recorded on-chain and paid by the platform. Completed windows stay listed below with winners and paid status as proof.
