How it works

  1. Enter a window - be on one of the plans the competition allows and pay the small entry once.
  2. Play inside the window - only verified wins made while it is live count. It stops taking results the moment it ends.
  3. Watch Final Points - Total Points boosted live by your plan (Level 3 = 1.5x, Level 2 = 1x). Drop to the free plan and you go hidden until you resubscribe.
  4. Top finishers share the pool - winners are recorded on-chain and paid by the platform; each window's page shows the final board and paid status.
