How can we help?

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Signing in

Use your email to sign in. A wallet is quietly created for you in the background, so you never have to deal with it. Your points and progress follow your account everywhere.

How do I win points?

Finish first in a match with a valid on-chain proof roll to earn +100 points. Points power levels and future competitions.

Still stuck?

Check the Forum first. Common questions are usually already answered there, and we can’t reply to emails about issues that are already covered in the forum.

If you can’t find an answer, we’re happy to help.

Email support

One tap opens your mail app. Expect a reply within 1–2 business days.
