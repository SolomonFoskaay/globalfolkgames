What GlobalFolkGames is

GlobalFolkGames is a growing library of the world's traditional and folk games - board games, dice games, word games, strategy games and family favourites from every continent. Each game is played true to its origins, and every match you finish is recorded on-chain, so results are verifiable and your playing record lives forever.

The games themselves are the point: the heritage, the strategy, the fun. The on-chain layer simply protects that experience with a permanent, checkable record and keeps your earned points safely in your own ledger. We are preserving native games for the world, one match at a time.

Alongside free play, a monthly Member Cup with real reward pools appears each month. Joining the cup is completely optional and only the members who qualify share that month's membership fees. Common questions

Is this hard to set up or play?

No. Sign in with your email, pick a game and play. The technical side runs in the background - you just enjoy the games.

How is my result verified?

Every finished match keeps a permanent on-chain signature. On the receipt explorer you can open any finished match and confirm the result yourself. For dice games the dice themselves are provably random.

What are Level 1 and Level 2?

Level 1 is the free way in: play, record your results and collect points at the base rate. Level 2 is an optional monthly plan that doubles your win points, lifts your daily reward to 200 points and gives you more lives each day. You can play at any level and switch any time.

What happens to my points?

Lifetime points stay on your ledger and never expire. Spendable points are what you use to enter optional competitions and buy boosts. Every entry and payout is recorded on-chain, so totals can always be checked.

How do competition windows work?

Each window has a set pool. Only results made while it is live count toward its leaderboard. When the window closes, the players at the top of the final board share the pool. Winners are recorded on-chain and paid by the platform, and each completed window stays listed with its winners and paid status as proof.
