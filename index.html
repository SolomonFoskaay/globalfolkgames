Common questions

Is this hard to set up or play?

No. The platform funds a wallet for you so all on-chain actions run without your pocket being touched. The technical side lives in the background; you just sign in and play.

How do I trust the dice?

Every roll resolves on the chain using a verifiable randomness source. Any roll can be re-checked on the receipt explorer by its signature, so the fairness is checkable, not just promised.

What are Level 1 and Level 2?

Level 1 is the free way in: play, win and collect points at the base rate. Level 2 is an optional monthly plan that multiplies your wins 2x, lifts your daily reward to 200 points and gives you more lives each day. You can play at any level and switch any time.

What happens to my points?

Lifetime points stay on your ledger and never expire. Spendable points are what you use to enter competitions and buy boosts. Every entry and payout is recorded on-chain, so totals can always be checked.

How do reward zones work?

Each window (daily, weekly, monthly) has a fixed pool. Inside a window, only wins made while it is live count toward the leaderboard. When the window closes, the top players share the pool. Winners are recorded on-chain and paid by the platform.

How does a roll get verified?

The roll is produced on-chain by a verifiable randomness machine, and the finish of every match keeps a permanent checkable signature. On the receipt explorer you can open any finished match and confirm the roll yourself, start to finish.

What happens to my personal details?

Only what you provide is used, and only for your account, payouts and support. The on-chain records carry no email or personal data; they are just verifiable proof tied to your game wallet.
