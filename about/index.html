Preserving the world’s folk games on-chain

GlobalFolkGames brings classic native games from around the world to your browser. Every match you play helps preserve a piece of cultural heritage, with real on-chain records proving every roll is fair.

Why we exist

Traditional games shaped how communities played and bonded for generations. We preserve them as living, playable experiences (not museum pieces) and prove their fairness on the Solana blockchain.

Fair by design

Every dice roll is provably fair, powered by MagicBlock's ER VRF on-chain. You get a plain, familiar web experience (sign in with your email and play). A wallet is quietly created for you in the background, and we handle all the blockchain costs, so games stay gasless and free.
