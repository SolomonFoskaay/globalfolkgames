Pay with crypto (USD only)

New to crypto? These five steps are all you need. You pay from your own GlobalFolkGames wallet, so support always knows it was you - only the wallet that paid gets the points.

1
Top up your wallet with a little SOL first.
Send at least 0.01 SOL (about $1-2) to your embedded Solana wallet on the site before you buy anything. You only do this once - it pays the tiny network fees on your future game transactions and renewals. Ask support (GlobalFolkGames@gmail.com) for your wallet address, or use the address shown on your profile.
2
Then fund it with USDC (or SOL).
For the actual plan or booster, send the needed USDC to the same wallet. USDC is the stable coin that stays equal to one US dollar, so your payment is a fixed USD amount - never a fluctuating guess.
3
Pay from the site, from your own wallet.
On this page you tap the exact plan you want and the site builds the payment from your embedded wallet. Because it pays from your own wallet, support always credits the correct account - no one else can claim your payment.
4
Confirm your payment.
You are taken to the confirmation page. Pick the plan you paid, add your wallet address and payment signature, and send it to support.
5
Points are credited to your wallet.
Support verifies within 24 hours and credits the premium points on-chain to the wallet that paid. Then you activate your plan or booster and play.
A note for total beginners:

Crypto can feel unfamiliar at first, but this flow is made to be friendly: your wallet lives on the site, you don't copy long addresses into outside apps, and you only ever pay with stable USDC in USD amounts. The 0.01 SOL top-up in step 1 is just enough for the tiny network fees, so future moves and renewals cost you almost nothing.
