Pay with crypto (USD only)

New to crypto? Follow the steps below. Your payment is tracked by your wallet address, so support always knows it was you - only the wallet that paid gets the points.

Your payment wallet (copy this)
Sign in to load your wallet.

This is your GlobalFolkGames wallet. It is the only wallet that can receive your payment and the only wallet the points will be credited to. If you do not see an address, open your Profile page and look for "Your on-chain wallet".

1
Get some crypto (the first-time step).
If you have never owned crypto, open an exchange (such as Binance, Coinbase, KuCoin, or any exchange available in your country), create an account, and buy a little SOL and the USDC you need. Already on crypto? Skip ahead - you can pay straight from any wallet you already have.
2
Copy your GlobalFolkGames wallet address.
Use the blue box above. Important: when you send from the exchange, paste this exact address and choose the network Solana at the destination. Choosing anything else (for example ERC-20 or BNB) sends your money to the wrong network and can be lost - so always pick Solana.
3
Send a little SOL first.
Send at least 0.01 SOL (about $1-2) to your GlobalFolkGames wallet. You only do this once - it pays the tiny network fees on your future game transactions. Without it, the chain cannot process your game moves.
4
Then send the USDC for your plan or booster.
Send the exact USDC amount for what you want (Level 1 = $5, Level 2 = $10, Level 3 = $15, 24h booster = $1, 72h booster = $2) to the same wallet address, always on the Solana network. USDC stays equal to one dollar, so your payment is a fixed USD amount - never a fluctuating guess.
5
Confirm your payment.
Go to the confirmation page, pick the plan you paid for, add your wallet address, and send it to support. They verify within 24 hours and credit the premium points on-chain to the wallet that paid.
6
Your wallet gets the points.
Only the wallet shown above can receive the points, so no one else can claim your payment. Once credited, activate your plan on the Upgrade page.
A note for total beginners:

Yes, you will copy your wallet address to the exchange - that is normal and safe. The address is public (like a bank account number), so sharing it just tells people where to send you money. Your private key never leaves your wallet. Remember: when sending to this address, always choose the Solana network.
