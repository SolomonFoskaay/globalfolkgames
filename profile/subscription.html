How to get a plan

  1. Tap Pay under your plan and complete the secure payment.
  2. You are redirected to the confirmation page - pick the same plan you paid for and send the receipt to support.
  3. Support verifies within 24 hours and credits your premium points on-chain.
  4. Go to your Upgrade page, refresh, and tap Activate to start your 30 days.

No auto-renew and non-refundable. Nothing charges again unless you choose it.

Common questions

Why do Level 2 and Level 3 differ in price?

Level 3 is the bigger boost: triple win points, the highest daily reward and lives, ad-free, and a 1.5x competition final-score multiplier. Level 2 is the lighter 2x step up.

Is this the price forever?

The launch beta discount is limited time. The regular price shows struck through on each plan. When the beta ends, the discount is removed.

Can I switch plans?

You run one plan at a time for its 30 days, then choose what to activate next - no auto-renew, so you are never charged on autopilot.
